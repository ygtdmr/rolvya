# Rolvya brand and repository positioning

## Approved identity

- **Name:** Rolvya; coined brand name, not a standard dictionary word or defined technical acronym.
- **Purpose:** open-source orchestration for AI coding agents with upfront dependency planning and safe parallel execution.
- **Approved short description:** “Open-source orchestration for AI coding agents, with dependency-aware task planning and parallel execution.”
- **Tagline:** the approved tagline is in the [repository README](../../README.md); display it **once per page when used**, rather than repeating it in headers and footers.
- **Visual:** previously accepted wordless geometric mark and navy, blue and gray palette; **no gold**. Do not redesign the accepted logo without an explicit request.

## Repository and growth

The source repository is public `ygtdmr/rolvya`, using `main` and GNU MIT. It is initially under the owner's personal account, with a possible future organization/company brand path; no migration is approved or implemented.

The owner expressed concern about third parties commercially repackaging the code. **MIT does not generally prohibit commercial use or competing distributions.** Do not impose incompatible commercial-use restrictions while calling the repository MIT; any trademark or separately licensed brand assets need distinct legal consideration. The logo and brand use terms are not currently specified by a separate policy.

## Writing guidelines

Use precise, restrained open-source technical language. Avoid repeated slogans and unsupported claims about shipping features. Documentation should be English, readable on GitHub and in Obsidian. Maintain approved name/casing and distinguish design proposals from working features.

## Open items

Official logo asset path and license, trademark/branding policy, organization migration, domain and website, social handle verification, contributor positioning and future commercial offering.
