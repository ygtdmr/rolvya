# S2-03B human sessions and voluntary TOTP

This Rust/SQLite library extends the merged [S2-03A primary-proof foundation](human-primary-authentication.md), PR #24, main `9710ff492e94605ac7d9fbc393347918d143a5b2`. It implements the session/factor boundaries of [ADR 0027](../decisions/0027-risk-based-session-management.md), [ADR 0028](../decisions/0028-human-session-token-safety.md), [ADR 0033](../decisions/0033-configurable-password-email-login-and-totp.md) and [ADR 0035](../decisions/0035-scoped-human-incident-containment.md). It does not implement a production login gateway or S2-03C roles/Owner approvals.

## Interfaces and authority

[`human_auth::sessions`](../../crates/persistence-sqlite/src/controller_keys/human_auth/sessions.rs) separates proof validation, session lifecycle and factors. `start_session` checks the immutable primary proof and its new installation-keyed browser-context sidecar; user, credential, email, policy and security versions are rechecked under `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`. Consumption and session creation (or restricted `PendingLogin`) commit together. Old S2-03A proofs without the sidecar cannot issue sessions. A pending factor flow grants no session authority. No ordinary authentication grants project membership, budget approval, worker authority or provider credential use.

Session handles have 256 OS-random bits. Only keyed, domain-separated commitments are stored; immutable family/token metadata is authenticated. `inspect_session`, `session_inventory`, `renew_session`, individual revocation, logout and all-device revocation derive the user from verified current credentials. Rotation advances one generation atomically. Concurrent renewals have one winner; old cookies never recover the winner's bearer. A dropped renewal response requires fresh primary authentication and the enrolled factor. There is no plaintext credential cache or grace-token resurrection.

The approved inactivity baseline is **seven days of real human inactivity**. Background polling, automatic renewal and worker activity do not advance it. Additional engineering defaults are 24-hour bearer lifetime, 90-day absolute family lifetime, 32 currently usable families and ten minutes between rotations after the first rotation. These bound exposure and append-only token growth; they are not new Owner-approved product rules or production-qualified thresholds. Expired, stale-epoch/policy and unavailable-email families do not strand a usable password route. No mandatory 30-day logout is introduced. Email-origin sessions conservatively deny access while operational mail evidence is unavailable; an enabled password route remains usable.

Private trusted request adapters must establish HTTPS, exact origin, request correlation, CSRF and human activity. The cookie contract is `__Host-rolvya`, Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, Path=/, no Domain. Outbound cookies, factor provisioning and recovery bundles use private response callbacks bound to their originating ceremony. There is no public bearer/seed getter or production adapter override. Ordinary public construction remains **default-deny**. A current stolen cookie remains a bearer risk; rotation alone is not theft detection.

## Voluntary factors and recovery

Exact-pinned **totp-rs 5.7.0**, with zeroization enabled, implements RFC 6238 SHA-1, six digits and 30-second timesteps. The bounded ±one-step window is an engineering default. Seeds contain 256 OS-random bits. Enrollment requires a current session plus a fresh primary proof bound to that exact session/generation, and activates only after a valid code. Activation increments the security epoch and revokes previous human sessions/proofs. Enrolled factors cannot be bypassed by switching primary methods.

Human-factor encryption reuses the Secret Broker's internal RustCrypto XChaCha20-Poly1305 operations and opaque KEK without inventing project connections or worker authority. Every factor has a fresh 256-bit DEK and separate 24-byte data/wrapping nonces. Canonical length-framed AAD binds installation, key/version, human identity, factor identity/version and operation domain. Wrap AAD also commits to the data envelope. Seeds, provisioning material and DEKs never enter SQLite/audit in plaintext.

SQLite serializes the last accepted timestep and pending-flow failures. Five failed submissions exhaust a flow; rolling account/trusted-client throttles remain durable. Recovery uses ten independently generated 128-bit one-use codes with keyed account/factor commitments. A recovery login still requires valid primary proof, revokes previous human sessions, retains the enrolled factor and records distinct recovery assurance. New code batches require a current TOTP-assured session, fresh scoped primary proof and fresh TOTP; old codes are revoked atomically. Lost delivery does not reconstruct plaintext codes. Factor removal and exceptional lost-factor/admin recovery remain unavailable pending reviewed action-bound/recovery ceremonies; no administrator bypass is added.

A separately verified incident can revoke the affected human session family and pending authority while preserving unrelated users/sessions. No IP-only theft conclusion or automatic global worker shutdown exists. Ordinary stale-generation use is rejected; comprehensive replay-investigation telemetry and a production incident evaluator remain future integration work.

## Persistence and verification boundaries

[Migration 0009](../../crates/persistence-sqlite/migrations/0009_human_sessions_totp.sql) adds sessions/generation history, primary context/use/blocks, encrypted factors, restricted pending logins, recovery verifiers, throttles and append-only redacted events. Published 0001–0008 are unchanged. The migration suite checks populated v8 human records upgrading to v9, and an injected v9 failure restores the original v8 guards before a successful retry. Forward guards permit confirmed optional factor activation but prevent identity/envelope substitution, replay rollback and silent factor removal. Historical security evidence is retained; an approved archival/retention implementation remains an operations gate. Indexed lookups, bounded inventory/active families and rotation throttling bound per-request work. No network operation or password hashing occurs in these write transactions.

The [focused tests](../../crates/persistence-sqlite/src/controller_keys/human_auth/sessions/tests.rs) use real SQLite/encryption and private synthetic transport grants, controlled clocks, concurrent writes, negative scope/integrity cases and restart. Ten actual SIGKILL/reopen cases cover before/after commits for initial issuance, rotation, factor activation, TOTP login and recovery-code consumption. Required DB/snapshot and available WAL bytes are inspected for seed, bearer, CSRF and recovery-code plaintext; substring checks do not prove absence from every memory/filesystem trace. Matching-key snapshots preserve encrypted factors and one-use state. A real admitted synthetic-worker fixture verifies human logout preserves existing financial reservations and worker credential authority.

Two independent read-only reviewers inspected actual session and factor code. Reproduced regressions cover original-deadline expiry during renewal, unusable families exhausting the cap, and recovery bundle substitution; request-scoped renewal delivery was also corrected. Exact commands/counts and final-head CI/CodeQL are recorded in the PR. Model reviews do not constitute Owner approval.

Production human enrollment, HTTPS/browser delivery, mail/delivery evidence, incident evaluation, factor removal and exceptional recovery remain disabled. No real browser behavior, SMTP delivery, physical power-loss resilience, production cryptographic certification, external provider or encrypted off-host recovery is claimed. Full controller compromise remains outside at-rest encryption protection. No live credentials, email or paid operations are used.

**S2-03C integration:** [current project roles and exact-action Owner authorization](human-owner-authorization.md) now add separate membership, fresh proof and single-transaction financial/governance effects. Ordinary session results still grant no Owner authority. Logout, rotation and scoped incidents invalidate affected pending governance authority without cancelling unrelated workers. The next security milestone after S2-03C review/merge is S2-04 worker authority.
